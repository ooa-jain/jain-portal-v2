Innovation and Emerging Areas — Definition

Definition: Programmes and Courses in Innovation and Emerging Areas are academic offerings that address new, evolving, or rapidly advancing domains of knowledge, skills, technologies, professions, and societal needs. Such offerings are characterized by their relevance to contemporary and future developments, interdisciplinary and multidisciplinary approaches, integration of research and innovation, responsiveness to industry and societal requirements, and alignment with global trends and national priorities. They may encompass emerging fields, transformative technologies, novel applications of existing disciplines, and innovative pedagogical approaches that prepare learners for future opportunities, challenges, and lifelong learning.

The Office of Academics is preparing a comprehensive University-level report on “Programmes and Courses in Innovation and Emerging Areas” in accordance with the latest UGC requirements. All Schools are requested to submit the required information for the academic years AY 2022–23 to AY 2026–27 in the prescribed format below.

Information to be Submitted

A. New Programmes Introduced (IN INNOVATIVE AND EMERGING AREAS)
  • New programme introduced (IN INNOVATIVE AND EMERGING AREAS)
  • Innovation/Emerging area addressed
  • New courses introduced
  • Unique programme features
  • Industry/Academic collaborations
B. Innovations in Existing Programmes
  • Curriculum revisions
  • New electives/minors/majors/specialisations
  • Interdisciplinary pathways
  • Experiential learning
C. Recognition by Global Professional Bodies
  • Accreditations
  • Recognitions
  • Certifications
  • Curriculum mapping
  • International benchmarking
  • Impact on student outcomes
D. Industry-Integrated Skilling
  • Industry-integrated courses
  • Embedded certifications
  • Internships/Apprenticeships
  • Skill-based modules
  • Joint delivery with industry
  • Industry assessment
E. Competency-Based Learning
  • OBE implementation
  • Competency-based curriculum
  • Capstone/Innovation projects
  • Field immersion/Clinical training
  • Assessment reforms

Supporting Evidence

  • BoS Minutes
  • Academic Council Approval
  • Curriculum & Syllabus
  • Programme Brochure
  • Industry MoUs
  • Professional Body Recognition
  • Assessment Rubrics
  • Student Project Reports
  • Relevant Web Links
Submission requirement: supporting evidence must be provided as a shared Google Drive link (with access granted to the Office of Academics) against each entry you submit in the tool. Where evidence is not yet available, note clearly what is missing so it can be tracked to closure.

General Instructions

  • Submit information only in the prescribed format (the tool below).
  • Ensure all information is complete, verified and evidence-based.
  • Provide supporting documents wherever applicable.
  • All submissions must be approved by the Dean/Head before submission.
  • For any clarification, Schools may contact the Office of Academics.
